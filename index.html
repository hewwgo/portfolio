          I'm a first-year PhD Fellow in Human-Centered Computing at Aarhus
          University, advised by Niklas Elmqvist. Background in cognitive
          science. Now working across HCI, AI, and design research.
        

        

          So far my PhD has covered
          creativity tools,
          collective knowledge systems,
          and agentic simulations
          — different entry points into the same question: what changes
          when AI becomes part of how people think, create, and collaborate?
